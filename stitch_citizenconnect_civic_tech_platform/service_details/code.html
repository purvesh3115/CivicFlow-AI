Universal Healthcare Access

A comprehensive municipal program ensuring every resident has access to essential medical services, preventative care, and emergency treatments regardless of socioeconomic status.

info Program Overview

The Universal Healthcare Access scheme is designed to bridge the gap in medical coverage for municipal residents. By partnering with local clinics, regional hospitals, and specialized care centers, the city provides a safety net for primary, secondary, and tertiary care.

Participants receive a digital health card that grants access to network providers with zero out-of-pocket costs for covered preventative services and significantly reduced copays for specialized treatments.

star Key Benefits

  • check Free annual wellness exams and essential screenings.
  • check Subsidized prescription medications at partner pharmacies.
  • check 24/7 access to telehealth nursing triage services.
  • check Mental health counseling up to 10 sessions annually.
