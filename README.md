# CivicFlow-AI
is an AI-powered civic technology mobile application designed to connect citizens, field officers, and city administrators on a single platform.

The application enables citizens to report civic issues, track complaints, interact with an AI assistant, and discover relevant government schemes and services. AI helps analyze complaint images and descriptions, identify issue categories, recommend departments and priorities, and detect potentially duplicate complaints.

Field officers can manage assigned complaints, update progress, upload resolution evidence, and handle issues in the field. Administrators can manage complaints, officers, departments, assignments, services, and overall civic operations through role-based controls.
